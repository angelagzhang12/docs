After signing up for ClassTrack, taking a few minutes to configure your profile and preferences ensures that deadline reminders, GPA calculations, and cross-device sync all work exactly the way you need them to. This guide walks you through every setting you should configure before adding your first class.

## Creating your account

ClassTrack offers three ways to create an account. Choose the method that works best for you — all three give you the same full-featured experience.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Email & Password">
    1. Go to [classtrack.app](https://classtrack.app) and click **Sign Up**.
    2. Enter your email address and choose a strong password.
    3. Click **Create Account**.
    4. Check your inbox for a verification email from ClassTrack and click the **Verify Email** link.
    5. Return to classtrack.app and sign in with your email and password.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Google Sign-In">
    1. Go to [classtrack.app](https://classtrack.app) and click **Continue with Google**.
    2. Select the Google account you want to use for ClassTrack.
    3. Review the permissions prompt and click **Allow**.
    4. You are signed in and your ClassTrack account is created automatically — no email verification required.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Apple Sign-In">
    1. Go to [classtrack.app](https://classtrack.app) and click **Continue with Apple**, or tap the same button in the iOS app.
    2. Authenticate with your Apple ID using Face ID, Touch ID, or your Apple ID password.
    3. Choose whether to share your real email address or use Apple's private relay email.
    4. Click **Continue**. Your ClassTrack account is created and you are signed in immediately.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Setting up your profile

Once you're signed in, navigate to **Settings → Profile** to complete your account information. Accurate profile details help ClassTrack personalize your experience and display the right context throughout the app.

- **Display name** — Enter the name you'd like ClassTrack to use when greeting you and in any shared features. This can be your full name, first name, or a nickname.
- **Institution / school name** — Enter the name of your high school, college, or university. ClassTrack uses this as a label throughout your account.
- **Current semester label** — Give your active semester a recognizable name, such as *Fall 2025* or *Spring 2026*. This label appears on your dashboard and GPA reports.
- **Semester start and end dates** — Set the exact first and last days of your current semester. ClassTrack uses these dates to scope deadline reminders and generate semester-level GPA summaries.

Click **Save Profile** after filling in your details.

## Choosing your grading scale

ClassTrack needs to know how your school converts letter grades to GPA points in order to calculate your GPA accurately. Navigate to **Settings → Grading Scale** to review and adjust the default mapping.

The standard 4.0 scale is pre-loaded by default:

| Letter Grade | GPA Points |
|---|---|
| A | 4.0 |
| A- | 3.7 |
| B+ | 3.3 |
| B | 3.0 |
| B- | 2.7 |
| C+ | 2.3 |
| C | 2.0 |
| C- | 1.7 |
| D+ | 1.3 |
| D | 1.0 |
| F | 0.0 |

If your school does not use plus/minus grading, toggle off **Enable plus/minus grades** in the grading scale settings. ClassTrack will simplify the scale to whole-letter grades only (A = 4.0, B = 3.0, C = 2.0, D = 1.0, F = 0.0). You can also manually adjust any GPA point value in the table to match your institution's exact scale.

## Syncing across devices

ClassTrack automatically syncs your classes, grades, deadlines, and settings across all platforms. As soon as you make a change on the web app, it appears instantly on your iOS and Android devices — and vice versa. There's nothing to configure for sync; it's always on as long as you're signed in to the same account on each device.

Download the ClassTrack app from the **App Store** (iOS) or **Google Play** (Android) and sign in with the same credentials you used on the web to get started on mobile.

<Tip>
  Set your **semester start and end dates** right away in **Settings → Profile**. ClassTrack uses these dates to time your deadline reminders correctly — without them, some notifications may not fire at the right intervals. It takes less than a minute and makes a big difference throughout the semester.
</Tip>

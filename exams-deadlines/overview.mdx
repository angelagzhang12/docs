Keeping up with exams, assignments, and project deadlines across multiple classes is one of the biggest challenges student life throws at you. ClassTrack's Exams & Deadlines feature brings everything into one place — so instead of juggling sticky notes, scattered calendar entries, and last-minute panic, you always know exactly what's coming up and when. From your first quiz of the semester to your final project submission, ClassTrack keeps your academic calendar organized and under control.

## What you can track

ClassTrack lets you log every type of time-sensitive academic event, not just exams. Use the categories below to keep your schedule complete and accurate.

## The Deadlines view

The **Deadlines** tab gives you a single, chronological list of every upcoming exam and deadline across all your classes. Rather than opening each course individually, you get one unified view sorted by date so the most urgent items always appear at the top.

Each item in the list is **color-coded by class**, making it easy to see at a glance which course an exam or deadline belongs to. You can also assign a **priority label** — High, Medium, or Low — to every item. Priority labels appear as colored badges next to each entry, helping you distinguish a final exam from a low-stakes reading response when your schedule starts to fill up.

## Calendar integration

If you prefer a visual layout, ClassTrack's built-in calendar lets you switch between a **weekly** and **monthly** view of all your exams and deadlines. Tap any date to see what's due, or tap an individual item to open its full details.

You can also export your deadlines to the calendar app you already use. ClassTrack supports one-tap export to **Google Calendar**, **Apple Calendar**, and **Microsoft Outlook**, so your academic schedule stays in sync with the rest of your life.

<Tip>
  Use priority labels strategically at the start of each week. Filter your Deadlines view to show only **High** priority items to build a focused to-do list for the days ahead, then revisit Medium and Low items once your most critical work is handled.
</Tip>

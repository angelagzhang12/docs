When you add every exam and deadline to ClassTrack at the start of the semester, your entire academic schedule becomes visible at a glance. Instead of discovering a midterm the night before or realizing a project is due tomorrow, you build an accurate picture of the weeks ahead — and ClassTrack surfaces what's coming up so you can plan your time with confidence.

## Adding an exam

Use these steps to add a new exam, quiz, or in-class test to ClassTrack.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Exams & Deadlines">
    Navigate to the **Exams & Deadlines** tab in the bottom navigation bar, or open a specific class and tap the **Deadlines** section within it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap '+ Add Exam'">
    Tap the **+ Add Exam** button in the top-right corner of the screen. A new entry form opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the exam details">
    Fill in the following fields:
    - **Exam name** — give it a clear, descriptive name (e.g. "Midterm Exam" or "Chapter 4–6 Quiz")
    - **Associated class** — select the course this exam belongs to
    - **Date and time** — enter the date and start time of the exam
    - **Location** *(optional)* — add the room number or building so you always know where to go
    - **Notes** *(optional)* — jot down topics to review, allowed materials, or any other reminders
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set a priority level">
    Choose a priority label — **High**, **Medium**, or **Low** — to indicate how much this exam affects your grade or requires your attention. High-priority items are highlighted throughout the app.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the exam">
    Tap **Save**. The exam immediately appears in your Deadlines list in chronological order and shows up on your ClassTrack calendar.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Adding a deadline

Follow these steps to log an assignment due date, project submission, or any other type of deadline.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Tap '+ Add Deadline'">
    From the **Exams & Deadlines** tab, tap **+ Add Deadline** to open the deadline entry form.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the deadline details">
    Complete the following fields:
    - **Deadline name** — describe the assignment clearly (e.g. "Research Paper Draft" or "Lab Report 3")
    - **Associated class** — link the deadline to the correct course
    - **Due date and time** — enter the exact date and time the item is due
    - **Type** — choose from **Assignment**, **Project**, **Paper**, **Lab**, or **Other** to categorize the deadline
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set priority and add notes">
    Assign a priority level and, if helpful, add notes — such as submission instructions, word count requirements, or links to rubrics.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the deadline">
    Tap **Save**. The deadline appears in your Deadlines list alongside your exams, sorted by due date.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Editing and completing items

To edit an existing exam or deadline, tap it in the Deadlines list to open the detail view, then tap **Edit** in the top-right corner. Update any field and tap **Save** to apply your changes.

When you finish an exam or submit an assignment, mark it as complete by tapping the **checkbox** to the left of the item, or swipe right on it and tap **Complete**. Completed items move automatically to the **Past** section at the bottom of your Deadlines list, keeping your active view focused on what still needs your attention. You can tap **Past** at any time to review or restore a completed item.

## Deadline fields reference

| Field | Description | Required |
|---|---|---|
| **Name** | A short, descriptive title for the exam or deadline | Yes |
| **Class** | The course the item belongs to | Yes |
| **Date/Time** | The exam date and start time, or the assignment due date and time | Yes |
| **Type** | Category of the item (Exam, Assignment, Project, Paper, Lab, Other) | Yes |
| **Priority** | Importance level: High, Medium, or Low | Yes |
| **Notes** | Free-text field for study topics, instructions, or reminders | No |
| **Reminder** | One or more notifications to alert you before the item is due | No |

<Note>
  **Premium feature:** ClassTrack can automatically import exam dates and assignment deadlines from your course syllabus. Upload a syllabus as a **PDF or plain text file** from the course settings page, and ClassTrack will scan it and populate your Deadlines list with detected dates — saving you the manual work at the start of each semester. Syllabus import is available on the ClassTrack Premium plan.
</Note>

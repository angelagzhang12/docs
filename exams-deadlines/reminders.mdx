A reminder the morning of an exam is useful, but a reminder several days before is what actually gives you time to prepare. ClassTrack's reminder system is built around that idea — nudging you early enough to study, draft, or plan rather than scramble. Whether you prefer a phone notification, an email, or a live countdown on your dashboard, ClassTrack gives you the flexibility to set reminders that match the way you work.

## Types of reminders

ClassTrack offers three complementary reminder types that you can use individually or together.

1. **Push notifications** — Alerts delivered directly to your mobile device via the ClassTrack app. Push notifications appear on your lock screen and notification center at the time you schedule them, so you never have to open the app to stay informed.

2. **Email reminders** — Reminder emails sent to the address associated with your ClassTrack account. Email reminders are useful as a backup channel or when you're working on a computer and not checking your phone.

3. **In-app countdown timers** — Live timers visible on your ClassTrack dashboard and on individual deadline cards. Countdown timers display the exact number of days and hours remaining until an item is due, giving you a constant sense of urgency without requiring a separate notification.

## Setting reminders for an item

You can attach one or more reminders to any exam or deadline you've already created. Follow these steps to configure them.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the exam or deadline">
    Tap the exam or deadline in your Deadlines list to open its detail view.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap 'Add Reminder'">
    Scroll down to the **Reminders** section of the detail view and tap **Add Reminder**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose when to be reminded">
    Select how far in advance you want the reminder:
    - **1 day before**
    - **2 days before**
    - **1 week before**
    - **Custom** — pick any specific date and time that suits your schedule
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the delivery method">
    Select how you want to receive the reminder:
    - **Push notification** — alert sent to your mobile device
    - **Email** — message sent to your ClassTrack account email
    - **Both** — receive a push notification and an email at the same time
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the reminder">
    Tap **Save Reminder**. The reminder appears under the Reminders section of the item's detail view, and you can see all scheduled alerts at a glance.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  You can add **multiple reminders** to a single exam or deadline. For example, set one reminder for 1 week before to start studying, and a second for 1 day before as a final check-in. Tap **Add Reminder** again after saving the first one to stack as many alerts as you need.
</Note>

## Default reminder preferences

Instead of configuring reminders one by one, you can set global defaults that automatically apply to every new exam or deadline you create. To update your defaults, go to **Settings > Notifications** and adjust the following options:

- **Default reminder timing** — choose the lead time (e.g. 1 day before) that gets applied automatically when you save a new item
- **Default delivery method** — set whether new items default to push notification, email, or both
- **High-priority auto-reminder** — optionally enable an extra reminder that fires 1 week before any item you mark as High priority

Your defaults apply to new items going forward. Existing exams and deadlines are not affected until you edit them individually.

## Countdown timers

The ClassTrack dashboard features a **Countdown Timer card** that always shows the time remaining until your next high-priority exam or deadline. The card displays a live countdown in days and hours, along with the item name and the class it belongs to.

Countdown timers also appear directly on deadline cards in the Deadlines list. Each High-priority item shows a small timer badge beneath its title so you can feel the urgency of upcoming items without doing any mental date math. Once an item is marked complete, its countdown disappears automatically.

<Tip>
  Don't just set a reminder for the day before an exam — add a dedicated **"start studying" reminder** several days in advance. For a midterm, try setting a custom reminder 5 days before with the note "Begin reviewing Chapter 1–5." This turns ClassTrack from a notification tool into an actual study planner.
</Tip>

<Warning>
  Push notifications require ClassTrack to have notification permissions enabled on your mobile device. If you're not receiving alerts, go to your device's **Settings > Apps > ClassTrack > Notifications** and make sure notifications are turned on. Without this permission, only email reminders and in-app countdown timers will be active.
</Warning>

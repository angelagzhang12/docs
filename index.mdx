## Get started in three steps

Whether you're brand new to ClassTrack or setting up for a new semester, getting started takes just a few minutes.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Sign up at [classtrack.app](https://classtrack.app) using your email address or with Google or Apple single sign-on. Your account is free to create and works across all your devices from day one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your classes">
    Enter each of your courses for the semester — including the class name, instructor, credit hours, and weekly schedule. ClassTrack uses this information to organize your deadlines and calculate your GPA accurately.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Track exams, deadlines & GPA">
    Log assignments, exams, and project due dates directly to each class. As you record grades, the GPA Tracker updates your semester and cumulative GPA automatically, keeping you informed of where you stand at all times.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  ClassTrack is available on **web**, **iOS**, and **Android**. Your data syncs automatically across all your devices, so you always have your schedule and grades at your fingertips.
</Note>

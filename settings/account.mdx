Everything related to your identity, security, and data lives in the Account section of ClassTrack Settings. You can get there by tapping the **gear icon** from any screen and selecting **Account**. From one place you can update your personal details, tighten your security settings, switch semesters, pull an export of your academic history, or — if you ever need to — remove your account entirely. This page walks you through each of those areas.

## Profile settings

Your profile tells ClassTrack who you are and how to tailor the experience to your academic situation. Navigate to **Settings → Account → Profile** to update any of the following fields:

- **Display name** — the name shown across the app and in any exported reports
- **Profile photo** — tap your current photo (or the placeholder avatar) to upload a new image from your camera roll
- **Institution / school name** — your university, college, or high school; appears on PDF exports for advisor sharing
- **Current semester** — the active semester ClassTrack uses to scope your dashboard and GPA calculations
- **Grading scale preference** — choose between a standard 4.0 scale, a percentage-based scale, or a custom scale you define; this setting affects how ClassTrack converts your grades into GPA points

Tap **Save** in the top-right corner after making any changes.

## Security

Keeping your account secure protects your academic records and personal information. ClassTrack gives you several tools to do that.

### Changing your password

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Security settings">
    Go to **Settings → Account → Security**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your current password">
    Type your existing password in the **Current Password** field to verify your identity.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set your new password">
    Enter your new password in the **New Password** field, then type it again in **Confirm New Password**. Your password must be at least 8 characters and include at least one number or special character.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the change">
    Tap **Update Password**. ClassTrack will sign you out of all other active sessions and send a confirmation email to your account address.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Connected accounts

If you signed up with **Google** or **Apple**, you can view and manage that connection under **Settings → Account → Security → Connected Accounts**. You can link or unlink a social sign-in at any time, as long as you have at least one active login method (a password or a connected account) remaining on your profile.

### Two-factor authentication (2FA)

For an extra layer of security, enable **two-factor authentication** from **Settings → Account → Security → Two-Factor Authentication**. Once enabled, ClassTrack will prompt you for a one-time code from your authenticator app each time you sign in on a new device.

## Semester management

ClassTrack is built around semesters, and managing them well keeps your GPA history clean and your dashboard focused on what's current.

- **Create a new semester** — tap **Settings → Account → Semesters → New Semester**, give it a name (e.g. "Fall 2025"), set its start and end dates, and tap **Create**.
- **Switch the active semester** — tap any semester in the list and select **Set as Active**. Your dashboard, class overview, and GPA summary will immediately reflect the classes and grades in that semester.
- **Archive a completed semester** — tap a past semester and select **Archive**. Archived semesters become read-only, so their grades can't be accidentally edited — but ClassTrack continues to include them in your cumulative GPA calculation, so your long-term academic record stays accurate.

## Exporting your data

You can download a complete record of your ClassTrack data at any time. Go to **Settings → Account → Export Data**, choose your preferred format — **CSV** for spreadsheet-friendly data or **PDF** for a formatted academic report — and tap **Export**. The file includes all your classes, grades, GPA history, and semester records.

The PDF export is particularly useful for sharing your academic progress with an advisor, since it formats your data into a clean, readable report that includes your institution name and grading scale.

## Deleting your account

<Warning>
  Account deletion is **permanent and irreversible**. Once you confirm deletion, all of your classes, grades, GPA history, semester records, and personal data are removed from ClassTrack's servers and cannot be recovered. If you're unsure, consider exporting your data first (see above) or reaching out to support before proceeding.
</Warning>

To delete your account, go to **Settings → Account → Delete Account**. Read the confirmation notice carefully, then enter your current password to confirm your identity and tap **Permanently Delete Account**. Your account will be deactivated immediately and fully purged within 30 days in accordance with ClassTrack's data retention policy.

<Note>
  If you have questions about data recovery, need help with a billing issue, or want to request a manual data export, contact the ClassTrack support team at **support@classtrack.app**. The support team can also help you pause or downgrade your subscription rather than deleting your account if that better fits your situation.
</Note>

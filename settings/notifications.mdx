Staying on top of deadlines and exams is much easier when ClassTrack reminds you at exactly the right moment. The Notifications settings screen lets you control which alerts you receive, how far in advance they fire, which channels (push or email) they use, and when you'd prefer not to be disturbed at all. Taking a few minutes to dial in these preferences means you'll never be caught off guard by a forgotten deadline — and you won't be buried in noise you don't need.

## Accessing notification settings

To open your notification preferences, tap the **gear icon** in the top-right corner of any screen to open Settings, then select **Notifications**. All of the options described on this page live on that single screen.

## Notification types

ClassTrack sends several distinct notification types, each targeting a different academic event. The table below summarizes what each type does and which delivery channels support it.

| Notification Type | Description | Channels |
|---|---|---|
| Exam reminder | Fires before a scheduled exam | Push, Email |
| Deadline reminder | Fires before an assignment or project due date | Push, Email |
| Grade entered | Confirms when a grade is logged | Push |
| GPA goal update | Alerts when your GPA crosses your goal threshold | Push, Email |
| Weekly summary | Recap of the week's upcoming items | Email |

You can enable or disable each notification type independently. Toggle the switch next to any type to turn it off without affecting the others.

## Default reminder timing

ClassTrack lets you set global default lead times so you don't have to configure a reminder every time you add an item. For example, you can tell ClassTrack to always remind you **2 days before any exam** and **1 day before any deadline**. To set your defaults, go to **Settings → Notifications → Default Timing** and choose a lead time for each item category.

Individual items can override these defaults. When you create or edit a specific exam or deadline, tap **Reminder** in the item's detail view to set a custom lead time for that item alone. The custom setting takes priority over your global default.

## Email notifications

ClassTrack sends email reminders to the address associated with your account. To verify or update this address, go to **Settings → Account → Profile** and check the email field there.

If you want to stop receiving a specific category of email — for example, weekly summaries — without turning off all email notifications, scroll to the **Email** section on the Notifications screen and toggle off just that category. Each email type has its own independent switch, so you stay in full control of your inbox without losing the alerts that matter to you.

Every ClassTrack email also includes an **Unsubscribe from this type** link at the bottom. Clicking it disables only that email category, leaving all other email notifications active.

## Do Not Disturb

If you'd rather not receive push notifications during sleeping hours or other focused periods, use the **Do Not Disturb** schedule. On the Notifications screen, tap **Do Not Disturb**, enable the toggle, and set your quiet hours — for example, **11 PM to 7 AM**. During that window, push notifications are queued and delivered once your quiet period ends. Email notifications are not affected by Do Not Disturb.

You can also activate Do Not Disturb manually at any time by tapping **Turn On Now** without setting a schedule, and then turn it off again when you're ready to receive notifications.

<Warning>
  Push notifications require explicit permission from your device's operating system. If you're not receiving push alerts, open your device's **Settings app**, find **ClassTrack** in your app list, and make sure **Notifications** are enabled. On iOS, also confirm that **Allow Notifications** is turned on and that your preferred alert style (banners, sounds, or badges) is selected. Without this device-level permission, ClassTrack cannot deliver push notifications regardless of your in-app settings.
</Warning>

<Tip>
  If you find push notifications disruptive but still want to stay organized, enable the **Weekly Summary** email. Every week you'll receive a clean digest of everything coming up — exams, deadlines, and assignments — without a single mid-day buzz on your phone. It's a low-noise way to plan your week every Sunday morning.
</Tip>

If something isn't working the way you expect, this page walks you through step-by-step fixes for the most common ClassTrack issues. Find the problem that matches your situation and follow the steps in order — most issues are resolved quickly without needing to contact support.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="My GPA looks wrong">
    An unexpected GPA is usually caused by one of a few easy-to-fix configuration issues. Work through each check below:

    1. **Confirm all classes have grades entered.** Classes with missing grades may default to zero or be calculated differently. Open each class and make sure every graded item has a value.
    2. **Verify your grading scale.** Go to **Settings → Grading Scale** and confirm the scale matches your institution's. Even a small difference (for example, whether your school uses plus/minus grades) can meaningfully affect your GPA.
    3. **Check credit hours.** Open each class and confirm the credit hour value is correct. A class entered with the wrong credit weight will skew your weighted GPA.
    4. **Look for classes excluded from GPA.** Go to each class's settings and check whether **"Exclude from GPA"** is toggled on unintentionally. Pass/Fail, Incomplete, or Withdrawn classes may also be excluded — verify these match your expectations.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I'm not receiving push notifications">
    If deadline reminders or exam alerts aren't arriving, work through the following steps:

    1. **Check device-level permissions.** Open your device's **Settings app**, find ClassTrack in the app list, and confirm that notifications are set to **Allow**.
    2. **Check ClassTrack's notification settings.** Inside ClassTrack, go to **Settings → Notifications** and make sure the specific notification type you're expecting (deadlines, exam reminders, etc.) is turned on.
    3. **Check Do Not Disturb.** If Do Not Disturb or Focus mode is active on your device at the time a notification should fire, it may be silenced. Review your scheduled DND windows in your device settings.
    4. **iOS only — enable Background App Refresh.** Go to your device **Settings → General → Background App Refresh** and make sure it's enabled for ClassTrack. Without this, the app may not be able to deliver time-sensitive alerts when it's running in the background.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="My data isn't syncing between devices">
    If changes you make on one device aren't showing up on another, try these steps:

    1. **Confirm you're signed in to the same account.** Open ClassTrack on both devices and check **Settings → Account** to verify you're using the same email address on each.
    2. **Check your internet connection.** ClassTrack syncs over the internet — make sure both devices have an active Wi-Fi or cellular connection.
    3. **Force-close and reopen the app.** On both devices, fully close ClassTrack and relaunch it. This triggers a fresh sync on startup.
    4. **Force a manual sync.** If the issue persists, go to **Settings → Account → Sync** and tap **"Force Sync Now"**. This pushes and pulls all data immediately.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I can't log in">
    If you're having trouble accessing your account, the fix depends on how you sign in:

    - **Email and password:** Tap **"Forgot Password"** on the login screen. You'll receive a reset link at your registered email address within a few minutes. Check your spam folder if it doesn't arrive.
    - **Google or Apple sign-in:** Make sure you're selecting the same social account you used when you first registered. Signing in with a different Google or Apple account will not find your ClassTrack data.
    - **Web browser:** If you're using ClassTrack on the web and the login page isn't behaving correctly, try clearing your browser's cookies and cache, then reload the page. You can also try a different browser to rule out a browser-specific issue.

    <Warning>
      If you've tried resetting your password and still can't access your account, [contact ClassTrack support](/help/contact) directly — do not create a new account, as this will not restore your existing data.
    </Warning>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="A class or deadline is missing">
    Before assuming data has been lost, check these common causes:

    1. **Check the semester selector.** Make sure you're viewing the correct semester on your dashboard. Classes and deadlines are organized by semester, so switching semesters will change what's visible.
    2. **Check your archives.** Items from past semesters or manually archived items won't appear in the active view. Go to **Settings → Semesters → Archives** to browse archived content.
    3. **Accidentally deleted items.** If you believe a class or deadline was deleted by mistake, [contact ClassTrack support](/help/contact) as soon as possible. Our team may be able to recover recently deleted items if you reach out **within 7 days** of the deletion.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The app is slow or crashing">
    Performance issues are usually resolved with one of the following steps:

    1. **Update ClassTrack.** Open the **App Store** (iOS) or **Google Play** (Android) and check for an available update. Running an outdated version is the most common cause of crashes and slowdowns.
    2. **Restart the app.** Force-close ClassTrack completely and relaunch it. This clears temporary memory and often resolves sluggish behavior.
    3. **Web users — clear your browser cache.** In your browser settings, clear cached files and cookies, then reload ClassTrack. If the problem continues, try opening ClassTrack in a different browser to determine whether the issue is browser-specific.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

If you've worked through the relevant steps above and your issue still isn't resolved, the ClassTrack support team is ready to help. Visit the [Contact Support](/help/contact) page to reach us by email or in-app chat.

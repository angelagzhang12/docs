We're here to help you get the most out of ClassTrack. Whether you've run into a technical issue or just have a question, reach out using any of the options below and we'll get back to you as quickly as possible — most email inquiries receive a response within one business day.

## Contact Options

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Email Support" icon="envelope">
    Send your question to **support@classtrack.app** and our team will respond within **1 business day**. Email support is available for all users, including free tier accounts.
  </Card>
  <Card title="In-App Chat" icon="comment">
    Tap the **chat bubble** in the bottom-right corner of the ClassTrack app to connect with the support team in real time. Live chat is available **Monday through Friday, 9 AM – 6 PM ET**.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Community Forum" icon="users">
    Visit **[community.classtrack.app](https://community.classtrack.app)** to ask questions, share tips, and get help from fellow ClassTrack users. The community forum is a great place for general how-to questions and study strategy discussions.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Feature Requests" icon="lightbulb">
    Have an idea that would make ClassTrack better? Submit it at **[feedback.classtrack.app](https://feedback.classtrack.app)**. You can also upvote suggestions from other users — the most popular ideas inform our product roadmap.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Before You Contact Us

<Note>
  Many common issues can be solved without waiting for a response. Before reaching out, take a moment to check the [FAQ](/help/faq) for answers to frequent questions, or visit the [Troubleshooting](/help/troubleshooting) page for step-by-step fixes to the most common problems.
</Note>

## What to Include in Your Support Request

The more context you give us upfront, the faster we can diagnose and resolve your issue. When you contact support, please include the following:

- **Your account email address** — the one you use to sign in to ClassTrack
- **What you were trying to do** — describe the action you took or the feature you were using
- **What happened instead** — describe the unexpected behavior, error message, or missing result
- **Your device and OS version** — for example, "iPhone 15 running iOS 17.4" or "Windows 11 in Chrome 124"
- **Screenshots, if helpful** — a screenshot of an error message or unexpected screen can significantly speed up diagnosis

<Tip>
  For the fastest possible response, use the **in-app chat** during business hours (Mon–Fri, 9 AM – 6 PM ET). Chat connects you directly with a support team member in real time, with no wait for an email reply.
</Tip>

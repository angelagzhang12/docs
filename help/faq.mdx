## General

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Is ClassTrack free?">
    ClassTrack offers a free tier that gives you everything you need to get started. On the free plan, you can track up to **6 classes per semester** and access all core features, including deadline tracking, grade entry, and GPA calculation.

    Upgrading to **ClassTrack Premium** unlocks:
    - Unlimited classes per semester
    - Syllabus import (auto-populate assignments and exams)
    - GPA goal tracking with progress forecasting
    - Data export (PDF and CSV)

    A **student discount** is available on Premium — verify your student status at checkout to unlock discounted pricing.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What platforms does ClassTrack support?">
    ClassTrack is available on the following platforms:

    - **Web** — access ClassTrack from any modern browser at [app.classtrack.app](https://app.classtrack.app)
    - **iOS** — download the app from the Apple App Store
    - **Android** — download the app from Google Play

    Your data syncs automatically across all platforms whenever you're connected to the internet, so you can switch between your phone, tablet, and computer without missing a beat.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I use ClassTrack for multiple semesters?">
    Yes. ClassTrack is built to follow you through your entire academic journey. You can create and manage multiple semesters, and switch between them at any time using the semester selector at the top of your dashboard.

    Your **cumulative GPA** is calculated automatically across all semesters, giving you an accurate picture of your overall academic standing no matter how many terms you've completed.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is my data private?">
    Your privacy matters to us. All of your academic data — including your classes, grades, and schedule — is **private to your account** and is never shared with third parties or your educational institution. ClassTrack does not sell your data or provide access to it to any outside organization.

    For full details, review the [ClassTrack Privacy Policy](https://classtrack.app/privacy).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I share my schedule with classmates?">
    Not currently. ClassTrack is designed as a personal academic planner, and schedule sharing is not available at this time. If you'd like to see this as a future feature, you're welcome to submit the idea at [feedback.classtrack.app](https://feedback.classtrack.app).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## GPA & Grades

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why doesn't my GPA match what my school shows?">
    GPA discrepancies are almost always caused by a difference in grading scales. Every institution uses its own scale, and ClassTrack lets you configure yours to match.

    To check your current scale, go to **Settings → Grading Scale** and confirm it matches your institution's official scale. A common source of mismatch: some schools do not award plus/minus grades (e.g., they give a flat "A" rather than "A+" or "A−"), which changes the GPA point values assigned to each grade.

    If you're unsure of your school's exact scale, check your institution's registrar website or academic handbook.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does ClassTrack count Pass/Fail classes in GPA?">
    By default, Pass/Fail classes are included in your GPA calculation. If your institution excludes them — or if you'd simply prefer not to count a specific class — you can turn this off on a per-class basis.

    To exclude a class from your GPA:
    1. Open the class from your dashboard.
    2. Tap the class settings (gear icon).
    3. Toggle **"Exclude from GPA"** to on.

    The class will remain visible in your schedule but will no longer factor into your GPA.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I handle incomplete or withdrawn grades?">
    ClassTrack supports both **Incomplete (I)** and **Withdrawn (W)** grade statuses. To mark a class with one of these statuses, open the class, go to its settings, and select the appropriate status from the **Grade Status** dropdown.

    Both Incomplete and Withdrawn grades are **excluded from GPA calculation by default**, which reflects how most institutions treat these statuses. You can review and adjust this behavior in Settings → Grading Scale if your school handles them differently.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I change a grade after I enter it?">
    Yes. You can edit any grade you've entered at any time. Tap the assignment or exam whose grade you want to update, edit the grade value, and save. Your GPA **recalculates instantly** to reflect the change — no need to manually refresh anything.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

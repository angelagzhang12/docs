ClassTrack is a student productivity app designed to eliminate the chaos of managing multiple courses, overlapping deadlines, and ever-shifting grade calculations. Instead of bouncing between spreadsheets, calendar apps, and paper planners, you get a single organized workspace where your classes, exams, assignments, and GPA all live together — updated in real time and accessible on any device.

## What ClassTrack does

ClassTrack is built around four core pillars that cover every aspect of your academic life, from scheduling to grade tracking.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Classes" icon="graduation-cap">
    Create a profile for each of your courses, complete with instructor details, credit hours, and a weekly class schedule. Everything related to a course lives in one place so you never lose track of what's due.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Exams & Deadlines" icon="calendar-check">
    Log assignments, exams, quizzes, and projects with precise due dates and times. ClassTrack surfaces your upcoming items by date so you always know what's next, and sends reminders before deadlines arrive.
  </Card>
  <Card title="GPA Tracker" icon="chart-line">
    Enter your grades as you receive them and let ClassTrack do the math. The GPA Tracker computes your semester GPA and cumulative GPA automatically, factoring in credit hours and your chosen grading scale.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Dashboard" icon="layout-dashboard">
    Your personal command center aggregates everything across all your classes — upcoming deadlines, recent grades, GPA trends, and schedule reminders — so you get a complete picture of your academic standing at a glance.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Who ClassTrack is for

ClassTrack is built for any student who wants to stay organized and informed about their academic progress. High school students use it to manage coursework and plan for college applications. Undergraduates rely on it to balance heavy course loads, extracurriculars, and part-time jobs. Graduate students appreciate the GPA precision tools and deadline tracking when juggling research, seminars, and thesis work. If you're enrolled in classes and want to stay on top of your performance, ClassTrack is for you.

## How it works

Getting value from ClassTrack takes just a few minutes of setup. Once your classes and grading preferences are configured, the app works continuously in the background to keep you organized.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add your classes">
    Start each semester by creating a class profile for every course you're enrolled in. Include the course name, instructor, credit hours, and meeting schedule so ClassTrack can organize your workload accurately.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Log assignments and exams">
    As syllabi are distributed, add your assignments, exams, quizzes, and projects to the relevant class. Set due dates and time estimates so ClassTrack can remind you before each deadline approaches.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Monitor your GPA">
    Each time you receive a grade, record it in ClassTrack. The GPA Tracker instantly recalculates your semester and cumulative GPA, giving you a real-time view of your academic standing and helping you identify which courses need more attention.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Stay on schedule with reminders">
    ClassTrack sends push notifications and email reminders based on your deadline preferences. You decide how far in advance you want to be notified — whether that's a week before a big exam or just a few hours before a homework submission closes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

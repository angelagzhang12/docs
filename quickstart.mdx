By the end of this guide, you'll have a ClassTrack account, at least one class on your dashboard, and a deadline or exam reminder already set up. The whole process takes less than five minutes, and you'll be equipped to track every class, grade, and due date for the rest of the semester.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Navigate to [classtrack.app](https://classtrack.app) and click **Sign Up**. You can register with an email address and password, or use **Continue with Google** or **Continue with Apple** for one-tap sign-in. Once you confirm your email (if signing up with email/password), your account is ready to use immediately. You can also download the iOS or Android app and sign up directly from your phone.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create your first class">
    From your dashboard, click **+ Add Class** in the top-right corner. Fill in the following details for your course:

    - **Class name** — for example, *Introduction to Psychology* or *CHEM 201*
    - **Instructor** — your professor's or teacher's name
    - **Credit hours** — the number of credits the course is worth (used for GPA calculations)
    - **Schedule** — the days and times the class meets each week

    Click **Save Class** when you're done. Your new class appears on the dashboard and in the sidebar under **My Classes**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add an assignment or exam">
    Open your newly created class by clicking its name. On the class page, click **Add Item** and choose one of the following item types:

    - **Assignment** — homework, essays, lab reports, and other graded work
    - **Exam** — midterms, finals, quizzes, and any timed assessments

    Enter a title, select the due date and time, and optionally add a description or reminder preference. Click **Save** to add the item to your class timeline and your main dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check your dashboard">
    Return to the main dashboard by clicking **Dashboard** in the sidebar. All upcoming assignments and exams across every class are listed here, sorted by due date from soonest to latest. Use this view as your daily check-in to see what needs your attention today, this week, and beyond.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Track your GPA">
    Once you receive a grade for an assignment or exam, open that item and enter your score. ClassTrack automatically converts your grade to the appropriate GPA points based on your chosen grading scale and recalculates both your **semester GPA** and **cumulative GPA** instantly. Visit the **GPA Tracker** tab in the sidebar to see a full breakdown by class and semester.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  The ClassTrack mobile app is available for free on **iOS** (App Store) and **Android** (Google Play). Sign in with the same account you created on the web and all your classes, deadlines, and grades sync instantly.
</Note>

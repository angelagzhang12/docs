The ClassTrack dashboard is your command center — the first thing you see when you open the app and the single screen that ties every part of your academic life together. From upcoming exams to live GPA tracking, the dashboard surfaces the information you need most without requiring you to dig through menus or switch between views. Whether you're planning your week or doing a last-minute check before class, the dashboard keeps you oriented and in control.

## Dashboard sections

The dashboard is organized into four widgets, each focused on a different dimension of your academic schedule. Together they give you a complete picture at a glance.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Upcoming Items" icon="clock">
    See every exam and deadline falling within the next 7 days, sorted chronologically. Items are color-coded by type so you can distinguish exams from assignments and projects at a glance.
  </Card>
  <Card title="GPA Summary" icon="chart-line">
    View your current semester GPA and your cumulative GPA side by side. The summary updates automatically each time you log a new grade, so your numbers are always current.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Class Overview" icon="graduation-cap">
    Get a quick snapshot of every active class this semester alongside your current grade in each one. Tap any class to jump directly into its detail view.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Countdown Timer" icon="hourglass-half">
    Stay aware of urgency with a live countdown to your next high-priority item — whether that's an exam tomorrow morning or a project due at midnight. The timer automatically advances to the next item once the current one passes.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Customizing your dashboard

You can rearrange the dashboard widgets to match the way you think about your schedule. On mobile, press and hold any widget, then drag it to a new position. On the web, click the **Edit Layout** button in the top-right corner of the dashboard to enter drag-and-drop mode.

To show or hide specific widgets, navigate to **Settings → Dashboard** and toggle each widget on or off. For example, if you prefer to track your GPA only from the dedicated GPA screen, you can hide the GPA Summary widget to keep the dashboard focused on upcoming items.

You can also switch between **light and dark mode** from the same **Settings → Dashboard** screen, or by tapping the sun/moon icon in the dashboard header. ClassTrack respects your device's system appearance setting by default, but you can override it here at any time.

## Quick actions

On mobile, a **"+" floating action button** sits in the bottom-right corner of the dashboard screen at all times. Tap it to instantly add a class, deadline, exam, or assignment without navigating away from the dashboard. A compact sheet slides up with just the essential fields, so you can log what you need in seconds and get straight back to what you were doing.

## Today's agenda view

When you only need to focus on the here and now, switch to **Today mode** by tapping the **Today** button at the top of the dashboard. This narrows the view to items that are due today or happening today — nothing more, nothing less. Today mode is especially useful on busy mornings when you want a clean, distraction-free list of exactly what's on your plate right now. Tap **All Upcoming** to return to the full 7-day view.

<Tip>
  Pin your most important or most demanding class to the top of the Class Overview widget so it's always the first thing you see. Open the Class Overview widget, swipe left on the class you want to prioritize, and tap **Pin to Top**.
</Tip>

Knowing your current GPA is useful — but knowing what it will take to reach your target GPA is even more powerful. ClassTrack lets you set a specific GPA goal and then work backward to understand exactly what grades you need in each class to get there. Whether you're chasing dean's list honors, maintaining a scholarship requirement, or simply trying to finish the semester stronger than you started, GPA Goals give you a concrete plan to follow.

## Setting a GPA goal

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Goal dialog">
    Go to **GPA Tracker**, then tap **Set Goal** in the top-right corner of the screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your target GPA">
    Type your target cumulative GPA — for example, **3.5**. You can set any value between 0.0 and 4.0.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the goal scope">
    Select whether this goal applies to your **current semester GPA** or your **cumulative GPA** across all semesters. Pick the scope that matches what you're working toward right now.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save your goal">
    Tap **Save Goal**. Your target immediately appears as a dashed reference line on your GPA chart and as a progress indicator on the main dashboard, so you can see at a glance how close you are.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What-if calculator

The what-if calculator lets you experiment with hypothetical grades to see how different outcomes would move your GPA — before a single real grade is changed.

To use it:

1. Open **GPA Tracker** and tap **What-if**.
2. Find the class you want to experiment with and change its projected grade to any letter grade or percentage.
3. Watch your semester and cumulative GPA update instantly in the preview panel.

You can adjust as many classes as you want in a single what-if session to model different scenarios — for example, "What if I get an A in Chemistry but only a C+ in Statistics?"

<Info>
  What-if changes are a simulation only. They are never saved to your actual grade record. When you close the what-if panel, all projected grades return to their real values and your displayed GPA reflects your actual performance.
</Info>

## Required grade calculator

The required grade calculator works in reverse: you tell ClassTrack the final grade you want in a class, and it tells you exactly what scores you need on your remaining assignments and exams to hit that target.

To use it, open any class, tap **Required Grade**, and enter your desired final grade. ClassTrack factors in the weight of every remaining gradable item — homework, quizzes, midterms, finals — and shows you the minimum score needed on each one. If the target is mathematically out of reach given the grades you've already earned, ClassTrack flags that clearly so you can adjust your goal to something realistic.

## Semester vs. cumulative goals

Understanding which type of goal to set helps you stay focused on what matters most right now.

- **Semester goal** — tracks your GPA for the current term only. Choose this when you have a short-term target, such as maintaining a 3.0 to keep a scholarship for the next semester.
- **Cumulative goal** — tracks your overall GPA across your entire academic record in ClassTrack. Choose this when you're working toward a long-term milestone, like graduating with a 3.5 or higher.

You can set both a semester goal and a cumulative goal simultaneously. ClassTrack displays them as separate reference lines on your GPA chart so you can monitor progress toward each one independently.

<Tip>
  Revisit your GPA goal at the midpoint of each semester — right after midterms. By then you have real grade data to work with, and there's still enough time left in the term to adjust your study plan, seek extra help, or recalibrate your target if your initial goal was too ambitious or not ambitious enough.
</Tip>

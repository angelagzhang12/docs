The GPA Tracker is ClassTrack's built-in academic performance hub. As soon as you log a grade for any assignment, quiz, or exam, ClassTrack recalculates your GPA instantly — no spreadsheets, no manual math. Whether you're checking in after a tough midterm or planning out the rest of your semester, the GPA Tracker gives you a clear, up-to-date picture of exactly where you stand academically.

## How GPA is calculated

ClassTrack converts your letter grades or percentage scores into grade points using your chosen grading scale, then weights each class by its credit hours. The result is a standard weighted GPA that reflects how demanding each course is relative to your total workload. You can choose between a standard 4.0 scale or a plus/minus scale, and you can customize percentage-to-letter grade cutoffs to match your school's grading policy. For a full walkthrough of the formula and scale options, see [Calculating GPA](/gpa/calculating-gpa).

## GPA history

The GPA history chart displays a bar for each semester you've recorded, so you can see your academic trend at a glance. Tap any semester bar to expand a breakdown of that term — including each class, its credit hours, and the grade you earned. This makes it easy to identify which semesters had the biggest impact on your cumulative GPA and to spot patterns in your performance over time.

<Note>
  Your GPA updates automatically every time you enter or update a grade. You never need to manually refresh or recalculate — ClassTrack handles it the moment you save a change.
</Note>

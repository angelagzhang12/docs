Every class in ClassTrack has its own dedicated assignment and grade tracker, so you always know what's due, what you've completed, and exactly where your grade stands. As you log assignments and enter scores throughout the semester, ClassTrack automatically recalculates your running average — giving you a real-time picture of your academic performance without any manual math.

## Adding an assignment

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the class">
    Tap the class card on your Classes screen to open the full class view.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap + Add Assignment">
    Tap the **+ Add Assignment** button in the Assignments section of the class page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the assignment details">
    Fill in the information for the assignment:
    - **Assignment name** — a clear title for the work (e.g., "Chapter 5 Homework" or "Midterm Exam")
    - **Type** — choose from Homework, Quiz, Midterm, Final, Project, or Other
    - **Due date & time** — when the assignment needs to be submitted
    - **Point value or percentage weight** — either the total points possible (e.g., 100 pts) or the weight it carries toward your final grade (e.g., 15%)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add notes (optional)">
    Use the **Notes** field to jot down any extra context, such as the topics covered, submission instructions, or a link to the assignment brief.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the assignment">
    Tap **Save**. The assignment appears in your class's assignment list and on your main deadline calendar, sorted by due date.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Entering grades

Once you receive a score for an assignment, mark it as graded so ClassTrack can factor it into your running average.

Open the assignment from your class page and tap **Enter Grade**. You can record your score in either of two formats:

- **Points** — enter your earned points out of the total possible (e.g., 87/100)
- **Percentage** — enter your score as a percentage directly (e.g., 87%)

After you save the score, ClassTrack immediately recalculates your **Current Grade** for the class, incorporating the new result into your overall average. If you need to correct a score later, simply reopen the assignment and update the grade field.

## Grade categories and weights

ClassTrack supports **weighted grading**, which lets you mirror the exact grading structure your instructor uses. Instead of treating all assignments equally, you can define grade categories and assign each one a percentage weight that reflects how much it counts toward your final grade.

To set up categories, open a class, tap **Edit**, and navigate to the **Grade Categories** section. From there you can create categories and assign weights, for example:

- Homework — 30%
- Quizzes — 20%
- Exams — 50%

Once your categories are configured, each assignment you create can be tagged to a category. ClassTrack uses those weights automatically when calculating your running grade, so the number you see always reflects your true standing in the course.

<Tip>
  Set up your grade categories at the very beginning of the semester, as soon as you have your course syllabus. Getting the weights right from day one means your running grade is accurate from the first assignment onward — no retroactive adjustments needed.
</Tip>

## Running grade

The **Current Grade** displayed prominently on each class page is your live running average across all graded assignments. ClassTrack recalculates it every time you enter or update a score. If you've configured grade categories, the calculation applies each category's weight accordingly; otherwise, it uses a straight average of all scored assignments.

Beneath your Current Grade, you'll also see a **Projected Grade** indicator. Enter a target score for any upcoming ungraded assignment, and ClassTrack will show you what your final grade would be if you hit that target — including how it would affect your GPA for the semester. Use the Projected Grade to set study goals and understand the stakes before a major exam or project deadline.

## Sample assignment breakdown

Here's an example of what a graded assignment list might look like for a single class:

| Assignment | Type | Points Earned | Points Possible | Grade % |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Chapter 1–3 Review | Homework | 47 | 50 | 94% |
| Quiz 1 | Quiz | 18 | 20 | 90% |
| Midterm Exam | Midterm | 82 | 100 | 82% |
| Research Project | Project | 88 | 100 | 88% |
| Final Exam | Final | — | 150 | — |

In the example above, the Final Exam has not yet been graded, so it does not affect the current running average.

<Note>
  Ungraded assignments do not affect your running average until you enter a score. Adding an assignment to ClassTrack only schedules it as a deadline — your Current Grade only changes when you record an actual result.
</Note>

The Classes feature is your central hub for managing every course in your semester. From the moment you add a class, ClassTrack keeps everything in one place — meeting times, instructor details, upcoming assignments, and your current grade — so you always know where you stand academically without juggling multiple apps or spreadsheets.

## Your class list

The main Classes screen shows all of your active courses for the current semester at a glance. Each class card displays the key details you need without having to open anything:

- **Class name** — the course title you entered when setting up the class
- **Instructor** — the professor or teacher associated with the course
- **Next meeting time** — the next upcoming session based on your configured schedule
- **Current grade** — your live running average calculated from all graded assignments

Tap or click any class card to open the full class view, where you can manage assignments, review your grade breakdown, and edit course details.

## Semester management

ClassTrack organizes all of your classes by semester, keeping your current workload separate from past terms. At the top of the Classes screen, you'll see the active semester label. Tap it to open the semester switcher, where you can:

- **Switch semesters** — jump to a previous term to review old grades and assignments
- **Create a new semester** — set up your upcoming term before it begins so you're ready on day one
- **Archive a semester** — move a completed term out of your active view while preserving all of its data for future reference

Archived semesters remain fully accessible; archiving simply keeps your active Classes screen focused on what matters right now.

<Tip>
  Assign a unique color to each class when you create it. Color labels appear on class cards, calendar events, and assignment lists, making it effortless to identify which course everything belongs to at a glance.
</Tip>
